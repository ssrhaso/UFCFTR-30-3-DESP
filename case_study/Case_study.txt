Use Case 2: Customer Multi-Vendor Order, Checkout and Payment
A customer browses or searches the marketplace and adds products from one or more
producers to their basket.
When the customer proceeds to checkout, the system checks that all selected products
are still available in the requested quantities. If an item is unavailable or there is
insufficient stock, the customer is informed and asked to amend the order.
If the products are available, the system identifies the producers involved and determines
the relevant delivery or collection arrangements. The customer reviews the order and
confirms the purchase.
The system then creates the overall marketplace order and separates it into the relevant
producer-specific order elements.
Payment is submitted to an external or simulated payment service. If payment is
successful, the order is confirmed, the appropriate inventory quantities are updated and
the relevant producers are notified.
If payment is declined, the customer is informed and given the opportunity to retry. If the
payment service is temporarily unavailable, the process should follow an appropriate
exception or retry path rather than simply terminating.
For multi-vendor orders, the system should calculate the amount attributable to each
producer and calculate the marketplace's 5% commission.
For assessment purposes, students should use a sandbox or simulated payment service
rather than a live payment system.
